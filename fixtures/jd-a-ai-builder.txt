AI Builder

About the role
AI Builders enter ambiguous enterprise problems with no defined solution, decide where
AI can create practical value, build something real, and explain their reasoning and
tradeoffs clearly. Strong AI Builders come from many backgrounds — software engineering,
UX, operations, consulting, research, product, and self-taught paths. What every strong
background shares is clear reasoning under ambiguity and constraint. We care more about
what you can actually build and ship than about pedigree.

What you'll do
- Translate an open, poorly-defined business problem into a concrete AI solution.
- Build working prototypes and production systems with modern AI tooling (LLMs, agents,
  RAG, orchestration), not slideware.
- Use AI with judgment: decide what to delegate to a model, verify and correct its output,
  and put guardrails/evals around it.
- Ship to real users and own the outcome end to end.
- Communicate tradeoffs, assumptions, and risks to non-technical stakeholders.

Hard requirements
- Demonstrated experience building real AI/automation systems that shipped and were used.
- Hands-on with LLMs and agentic/automation tooling.
- Evidence of strong individual ownership — you built it, not just attended the project.
- Clear reasoning under ambiguity.

Preferred
- Experience in regulated or high-volume enterprise operations.
- Track record of measurable outcomes (time saved, productivity, adoption).
- Comfort using AI tools to build, with transparency about how AI was used.

Not required
- A specific degree or school. Academic credentials are a minor signal at most.
