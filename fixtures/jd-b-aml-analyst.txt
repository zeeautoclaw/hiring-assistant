AML / Financial Crime Compliance Analyst

About the role
We are hiring an Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) and financial-crime compliance analyst to
investigate alerts, perform KYC/CDD reviews, and prepare audit-ready case files in a
heavily regulated banking environment. This is a domain-expertise role: depth in
compliance process, regulatory rigor, and investigative quality matter most.

What you'll do
- Investigate transaction-monitoring and sanctions alerts; dispositions with full rationale.
- Conduct KYC / customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence reviews.
- Produce regulator-ready documentation that passes audit on the first pass.
- Apply AML/KYC regulations, typologies, and internal SOPs consistently.
- Work high alert volumes without sacrificing quality.

Hard requirements
- Direct AML / KYC / financial-crime investigation experience in a regulated institution.
- Strong knowledge of AML regulation, sanctions screening, and case documentation standards.
- Track record of high-quality, audit-passing compliance work at volume.
- Meticulous, process-disciplined, detail-oriented.

Preferred
- Bilingual documentation ability.
- Experience improving investigation turnaround or analyst productivity.
- Familiarity with compliance tooling and case-management systems.

Not the focus
- Building novel software or AI systems is not the core of this role.
