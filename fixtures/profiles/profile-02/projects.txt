PROJECT 1 — AML investigation automation suite (Scotiabank)

What it is: a set of automations that take routine steps out of an AML investigator's day —
pulling customer data, pre-filling case templates, and routing alerts.

What I personally did: I mapped the existing SOP step by step, identified which steps were
mechanical, and built scripts to handle those while leaving judgment calls to the analyst.
Outcome: investigator throughput went from ~1 to ~2 cases/day, and the automated steps saw
roughly a 90% time reduction. I owned the SOP mapping and the rollout to the team.

How I used AI: I used LLMs to draft the first-pass narrative of each case, but every
narrative is reviewed by a human investigator before filing — regulators require it, and
the model occasionally misstated a typology. So AI drafts, human approves.

PROJECT 2 — 8-source compliance screening pipeline

A screening pipeline that checks an entity across 8 public/registry sources and produces a
20-column bilingual (EN/中) risk spreadsheet, with province-aware routing for Canadian
jurisdictions. Built with UI.Vision + Python/Playwright. I designed the column schema with
the compliance team so the output maps to what an auditor expects.

PROJECT 3 — PwC AML automation portfolio

Across 20+ engagements I introduced automation into investigation workflows, cutting

Manila) and was accountable for quality and audit-readiness of the output.
