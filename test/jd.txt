APPENDIX A: AI Builder Job Description –Senior Consultant
Overview
At KPMG in Canada, our people bring their unique perspectives to Canada’s most important
challenges. Here, you can build momentum that reaches beyond our business, develop skills for
the future, and take ownership of your career with support at every stage. Join a firm where your
career can make a difference.
KPMG in Canada is scaling an internal team of AI Builders who operate as the engine
embedded in the business—powering the firm’s agent builds and transforming our business
This team is for people who sit at the intersection of enterprise operations, technology, and
design, and who can look at how the firm works today, imagine how it should operate with
agentic AI, and make that future real through execution.
Our objective is to move beyond experimentation and enable real internal adoption of agentic
AI, redesigning core workflows, decision systems, and operating models using autonomous
agents that power KPMG’s service lines and internal teams.
Within this role, AI Builders take ownership of the full build lifecycle, working closely with
Engineering and Internal Transformation teams to move solutions from our KPMG AI Lab into
scalable, reliable production systems, all within a complex and regulated enterprise.
What you will do
At the Senior Consultant level on this team, you will be a hands-on AI Builder who takes
ownership of well-scoped internal problems and contributes to building real, agentic solutions
end to end. You will work closely with senior builders, practice leads, and internal stakeholders
on rapid experimentation, prototyping, and Lab-based initiatives that demonstrate and validate
new agentic capabilities.
In this role, you will:
• Build, test, and harden agents, plugins, skills, and tools using the firm’s approved
technology stack, working in deep connection with cloud and engineering teams to
deliver production-grade solutions.
• Own the full build lifecycle: prototype in the AI Lab, productionize through Internal
Transformation, and maintain solutions post-deployment with support from engineering
and platform teams.
• Work with production systems, design evaluations, and handle integrations with
enterprise platforms and data sources.
• Contribute reusable skills, components, and tools back into the shared AI stack so each
subsequent build starts further ahead.
• Operate inside the shared builder pool, deployed against the highest-leverage agent
builds across the organization.
• Be an active member of the AI Lab, contributing demos, reusable assets, and helping
build a strong builder community across the firm.
What you bring to the role
At KPMG in Canada, we know the strongest builders come from a wide range of backgrounds,
including engineering, business, strategy, design, and self-taught technical paths. This role is
designed for early-career professionals, who are ready to build, learn, and grow quickly in a
complex and regulated enterprise environment.
• You bring enterprise fluency and understand how organizations operate.
• You can identify real pain points, constraints, and opportunities where agentic AI creates
tangible value.
• You think in workflows, not just models, designing multi-step processes that account for
human-AI handoffs, decision boundaries, and appropriate levels of autonomy.
• You have a builder mindset and don’t just describe solutions, you create them, whether
through code, configuration, low-code platforms, APIs, or orchestration tools.
• You take end-to-end ownership, from problem definition through to a working system
that people actually use.
• You’re comfortable operating in ambiguity and understand that enterprise transformation
is rarely linear.
• You can make reasonable assumptions, build quickly, test, learn, and iterate. You also
apply sound judgment, treating risk, governance, ethics, and trust as core design
constraints rather than afterthoughts.
Where and how you’ll work
You will be based out of KPMG’s AI Labs in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, or Montreal
As part of the internal builder model, your work will align primarily the following context:
Embedded in business units (AI Builder – Internal): Supporting internal teams by building
and testing agentic workflows and prototypes that explore how autonomous agents could
improve internal delivery and operations. You’ll work alongside a core internal team and gain
exposure to a range of internal use cases, tools, and applied research efforts across the firm.
What’s in it for you
• The opportunity to work on genuinely new agentic AI use cases at the experimentation
and prototyping stage.
• Exposure to complex, regulated, enterprise-scale internal problems and workflows.
• Hands-on experience building demos, prototypes, and showcase artifacts that influence
how AI is adopted internally.
• A multidisciplinary environment with builders, researchers, designers, and business
professionals working side by side.
• An AI Lab built for experimentation, with secure infrastructure, frontier models, and
applied research.
• Access to frontier models and platforms, including early exposure to leading
foundation models, agent frameworks, and enterprise-grade AI tooling through KPMG’s
secure AI Lab environment, enabling hands-on experimentation, prototyping, and
applied learning at the forefront of agentic AI.
